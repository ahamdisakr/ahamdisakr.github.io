Brightspace Quiz Generator

Credit: This generator was developed by Algonquin College to make it easy to create question sets you can import into Brightspace quizzes and pools.

Instructions

  1. All questions must follow a proper format for the generator to recognize them.
  2. Select the Question Type Samples dropdown and click Create to insert a sample question.
  3. Copy/paste or type your questions in the editor below.
  4. Click Generate Test Questions.
  5. If generation fails, any invalid questions will be highlighted in red—hover to see the error.
  6. After a successful check, click Download Questions for Brightspace.
  7. In Brightspace (Add/Edit Questions), use Import, upload the CSV, and follow the prompts.
