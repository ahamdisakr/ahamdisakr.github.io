Convolution: Flip and Slide

The output of an LTI system is the convolution of the input with the impulse response, y(t) = ∫ x(τ) h(t-τ) dτ. To find one value y(t): flip h(τ) to get h(-τ), slide it by t, multiply it by x(τ), and add up the area under the product. Doing this for every t traces out the whole output. In discrete time the area becomes a sum, y[n] = Σ x[k] h[n-k].

Try it: press Play and watch the output change only while the two signals overlap. A pulse convolved with a pulse gives a triangle, and the output lasts as long as the two durations added together. Then flip x instead of h: the picture changes, but the output does not, because x*h = h*x. Finally, switch to discrete time to see the same steps with samples.
