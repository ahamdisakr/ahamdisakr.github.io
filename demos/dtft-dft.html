DTFT and DFT: Windowing, Leakage and Zero-Padding

A computer only ever has a finite number N of samples, so it works with a windowed sequence w[n] x[n]. Its DTFT is a continuous function of the frequency f in cycles per sample, periodic with period 1. The DFT of length M computes M samples of that DTFT, at f = k/M. Padding the sequence with zeros (M > N) samples the same DTFT more densely; only more data (a larger N) or a different window changes its shape.

X(ej2πf) = Σ w[n] x[n] e−j2πfn (n = 0…N−1), X[k] = X(ej2πk/M), k = 0…M−1

Try it: with one cosine, set f0 = 0.125, N = 32 and no zero-padding. That is exactly 4 cycles in 32 samples, so the DFT has only two nonzero samples, at k = 4 and k = 28, even though the DTFT has side lobes: the other samples land on its zeros. Press Sweep: as f0 moves between bins, the energy leaks into many DFT samples and the tallest one drops by up to about a third with the rectangular window, worst halfway between bins. Raise the zero-padding to 8N: the samples now trace the DTFT, whose shape has not changed. Choose the two close cosines (Δf = 0.04): at N = 32 there are two peaks; lower N to 16 and they merge, since separating two tones needs about Δf > 1/N. Finally choose the strong and weak cosines and the dB scale, and compare the windows: the side lobes of the rectangular window hide the weak tone, and the lower side lobes of the Hann window reveal it, at the cost of a wider main lobe. Predict, then check

Two cosines are 0.02 cycles/sample apart and merge into one peak at N = 32. Does zero-padding to M = 8N separate them?

No. Zero-padding only samples the same DTFT more densely, and that DTFT has a single merged peak. To separate them you need a main lobe narrower than the spacing, so more data: about N > 1/Δf = 50 with the rectangular window, twice that with Hann.

With N = 32 and no zero-padding, where does f0 have to be for the DFT to show no leakage at all?

On a bin: f0 = k/32, a whole number of cycles in the window. Then every other DFT sample falls on a zero of the DTFT. The leakage is still there in the DTFT; the DFT just samples it where it is zero.
