Open Positions

PhD Students

We are looking for motivated PhD students with background in:

  • Wireless and vehicular communications
  • Cooperative and autonomous driving
  • Machine learning and artificial intelligence
  • Edge computing and distributed systems

Applicants should possess prior research experience, a solid academic track record, and strong programming skills.

Master's Students

Opportunities for thesis-based Master's students in Electrical and Computer Engineering. Applicants should possess a solid academic track record.

Undergraduate Students

We welcome undergraduate students for research internships and project collaborations. You are highly encouraged to apply for the Outstanding Scholars Program at the University of Windsor and seek my support for your application. If interested, contact me via email for further details, and include your transcript.

The following individuals are also encouraged to apply:

  • Self-funded visiting faculty from other institutions.
  • Postdoctoral fellows with a solid research background.
  • Domestic graduate applicants and current MEng students seeking to transfer to an MSc degree.
  • Current undergraduate students at the University of Windsor who are in their last year with a GPA higher than 80%.

How to Apply

Interested candidates should email Dr. Sakr with all of the following:

  • A current CV
  • Academic transcripts (unofficial copies are acceptable initially)
  • A brief statement of research interests and goals
  • Your preferred start date for your studies (e.g., Jan., May, or Sept.)
  • Funding requirement (e.g., no, partial, or full financial support)
  • For PhD applicants, sample publications (if available)

Please use the subject line: "Prospective Student Application for [Position]"

Please submit documents in PDF format and avoid using links to personal cloud folders.

As I receive a substantial volume of emails, please allow for some time for a response. Depending on my workload, this may take a few weeks, so please be patient. Repeatedly contacting me to inquire about the status of your application or sending mass emails may lead to your messages being overlooked.
